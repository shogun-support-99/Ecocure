01 — Colour

Paper, ink,
moss, clay.

Four printed values, nothing else. Tints are mixed from the ink token, so a rule or a caption can never drift away from the palette. Moss carries labels and chapter numbers; clay is reserved for italic emphasis and nothing functional.

  • #F4EFE2 paper — page ground
  • #1A1C13 ink — type, rules, buttons
  • #4D6330 moss — labels, index
  • #9A4F2B clay — italic emphasis

03 — Plate, as component

A print, a mat,
an opening.

The same three elements in three states. Only --plate-open changes: mat and border collapse, the ratio turns from 4:3 print to a wide window, the sepia lifts to full colour, the printed caption gives way to a label on the image.

04 — The opening, on scroll

The plate
becomes the page.

The component pinned for one viewport. Scroll through and the print opens edge to edge on its own — no video, no canvas, one custom property written from scroll position. This is the transition the hero and each chapter will use.

plate open 0.00 · matted print

Held back on purpose

No section has been
rewritten yet.

  • index.html, css/style.css and the 3D world are untouched. This page and the two new files are additive.
  • No Google Fonts in production. Fraunces, Instrument Sans and Martian Mono are served from /fonts as 5 woff2 files.
  • The plate is code, not a composite. States are driven by one property; the scroll opening is js/plate.js reading position.
  • Not here yet: the chapter layouts, the index table, the field-note panel, motion beyond the plate, and any 3D-to-plate handoff.

Approve the palette, the reading sizes and the opening — or name what to change. Phase 2 rebuilds the hero and the first chapter plate against this layer.
